I'm Sakib — an ICT Laboratory Engineer at Karelia University of Applied Sciences, with a Bachelor's degree in Internet of Things from Savonia University of Applied Sciences.

Since August 2026 I've been responsible for the technical maintenance, development and operational support of Karelia's ICT laboratory and Nutanix teaching data center — servers, networks and workstations, security and patching, and the VMs teachers and students work on every day.

More and more of that infrastructure now runs local AI. I've set up Lenovo ThinkStation PGX workstations with Ollama, Open WebUI and Qdrant, I'm building the SilverARC AI agent on a private local-LLM stack, and I'm working on Karelia's AI Lab — a closed environment where companies, students and researchers can test AI on sensitive data.

Before that, my work at Savonia sat at the intersection of industrial automation, cybersecurity, and smart infrastructure. I've built OPC UA systems for critical infrastructure at Savonia's WaterLAB, developed post-quantum cryptographic tunnels under the Business Finland funded AIQUSEC project, and built digital twin models — from Ignition HMI dashboards to full 3D Unity environments.

Beyond the technical work, I care about making tomorrow's connected systems not just smarter, but provably safer.
